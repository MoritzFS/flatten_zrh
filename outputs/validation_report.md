# Validation report

Zurich's checks follow the four upstream (flattensf) runs for San Francisco, against Swiss references. Every figure below is generated by `python -m zrh_flat_routes validate`.

## 1. Elevation: swissALTI3D (2 m) vs the independent DHM25 (25 m)

- Points compared: **4,000**, random, on land inside the city
- Mean difference: **-0.03 m**, median **+0.02 m**
- RMS difference: **1.97 m**
- Within 2 m: **79.7%** of points; within 5 m: **97.0%**

DHM25 was interpolated from the contour lines of the 1:25,000 National Map and shares no lidar with swissALTI3D, so a small mean difference confirms the mosaic is correctly georeferenced and in metres above sea level (LN02). Residual scatter is expected: swisstopo gives DHM25's own average deviation as 1.5 m on the Central Plateau, its 25 m grid cannot resolve street-scale relief, and it predates much of the city's construction (railway cuttings, the Hardbrücke, the Europaallee site).
